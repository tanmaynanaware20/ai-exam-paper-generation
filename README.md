# 🎓 EXAM-AI

### AI-Powered Examination & PYQ Intelligence Platform

> **Turn Previous Year Question Papers into actionable exam intelligence.**

🌐 **Live Demo:** https://ai-exam-paper-generation.vercel.app/

EXAM-AI is a full-stack AI-powered examination platform designed for
engineering colleges, universities, professors, and students.

It analyzes Previous Year Question Papers (PYQs), identifies repeated and
important questions, generates **30-mark and 70-mark university-style
question papers**, conducts online tests, and evaluates subjective student
answers using **OpenRouter LLMs**.

---

## 🌟 Why EXAM-AI?

Students often study hundreds of questions without knowing which topics
are actually important.

Teachers also spend significant time:

- Analyzing previous-year papers
- Identifying repeated questions
- Creating question papers
- Preparing online tests
- Checking subjective answers
- Tracking student performance

EXAM-AI brings these workflows together into a single AI-powered platform.
